Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information CustomCloset ("we", "us") collects when you use our website, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information you provide at checkout: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and pincode. If you pay via UPI, you upload a screenshot of your payment confirmation, which we use solely to verify and process your order.

If you use the Design Studio, any image or artwork you upload is used to generate your custom product preview and is shared with us so we can print your order.

If you write a product review or a custom-order review, we store the name, rating, review text, and optional photo you submit, and display it publicly on the relevant page.

Your shopping cart is stored locally in your browser (not on our servers) using your browser's local storage, so it stays intact if you leave and come back.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, confirm, and fulfill your order
  • To communicate with you about your order, primarily over WhatsApp
  • To display reviews you've chosen to submit publicly on the site
  • To improve our products and site based on general order patterns

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-party services we use

To run this site, we rely on a small number of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — stores order details and reviews in a secure database.
  • Cloudinary — stores product photos, payment screenshots, and images you upload.
  • WhatsApp — used to communicate order confirmations; messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their function and are not authorized to use it for their own purposes.

4. Payment screenshots

Payment screenshots you upload at checkout are automatically deleted from our systems 15 days after the order is placed. We keep them only long enough to verify and reconcile payments.

5. Data retention

We retain order records (name, contact details, order history) for as long as necessary for accounting, customer service, and legal record-keeping purposes. Reviews remain visible on the site until you request removal or we remove them at our discretion.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you — including removing a review you've posted — by emailing customcloset111@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart contents and to remember that you've seen this policy — we don't use tracking cookies for advertising.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 18. If you're under 18, please use this site only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our business or the tools we use change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at customcloset111@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 8766948989.
